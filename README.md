# Quasario — Front-End

Quasario is a centralized digital platform designed to connect users with local businesses, service providers, and organizations through organized and accessible digital profiles.

The platform aims to help users discover businesses and services, explore their work, find offers and events, and communicate with service providers through one centralized platform.

## Front-End

This repository is dedicated to the **Front-End development of the Quasario project**, which is being developed by the Front-End team.

The Front-End will focus on creating an organized, user-friendly, and responsive interface for the different users and business types supported by the platform.

## Planned Features

* Business and service provider profiles
* Search and filtering interfaces
* Location-based business discovery
* Portfolio and previous-work presentation
* Offers, discounts, and events
* Ratings and reviews
* Customizable sections for different business types
* User account and profile interfaces
* Responsive user interfaces

## Project Goal

Quasario aims to solve the difficulty of finding and communicating with small businesses and service providers through scattered and unorganized communication channels.

The platform will provide a centralized digital space where businesses can present their services professionally, while users can discover and access relevant services more easily.
